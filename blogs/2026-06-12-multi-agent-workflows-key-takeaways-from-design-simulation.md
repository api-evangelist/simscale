What happens when a design review agent and a simulation agent can actually coordinate across platforms? That’s what Jon Wilde, VP of Product at SimScale, and Jeremy Andrews, Co-Founder and CTO of CoLab, demonstrated live at Design & Simulation Week 2026, hosted by engineering.com. Between them, they covered why AI has transformed software engineering but stalled in hardware, where simulation time actually gets lost, and what a working proof of concept of two AI agents talking to each other looks like in practice.
