What happens when you replace the simulation bottleneck with an AI that thinks like your best engineer? That’s the question at the heart of SimScale’s latest webinar, featuring Jon Wilde, VP of Product at SimScale, and Armin Narimanzadeh, Manager of Thermofluids & Simulations at Convion. Between them, they covered everything from why AI has conquered software but stalled in hardware, to how one engineering team compressed a months-long design optimization into under an hour — and found a geometry they never would have thought to look for.
