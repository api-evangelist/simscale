At SimScale, our mission is to empower engineering teams everywhere to innovate faster by exploring thousands of engineering decisions in seconds. That means shipping capabilities that cut simulation lead time, remove setup friction, and expand the physics you can access from a single browser tab. This summer, the updates center on Engineering AI, the PAMICS® SPH solver integration and important electromagnetics capabilities for modeling motion.
